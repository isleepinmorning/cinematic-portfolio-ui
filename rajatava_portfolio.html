About Me

"I don't just design — I craft experiences that leave a mark."

I'm Rajatava Sarkar, a 21-year-old graphic designer from Cooch Behar, India. My work lives at the intersection of bold visual storytelling and refined aesthetic precision. I believe every pixel should have purpose, every color should carry emotion, and every design should tell a story worth remembering.

From brand identities to social campaigns, I craft visuals that don't just look good — they make people feel something. My design philosophy is simple: beauty with intent, creativity with structure, and always, always originality.

How I Work

THE PROCESS

01

Discover & Understand

Every great design starts with deep listening. I immerse myself in your world — your brand, your audience, your ambitions. Understanding the story you want to tell is the foundation of everything I create.

02

Concept & Ideate

Where constraints become creative fuel. I explore directions that go beyond the obvious, pushing boundaries while keeping your vision at the center. This is where bold ideas are born.

03

Design & Refine

Execution with obsessive precision. Every element is crafted with intention — typography chosen for its voice, color for its emotion, layout for its impact. I refine until every detail is perfect.

04

Deliver & Impact

The final design is more than a file — it's a living brand asset ready to make its mark on the world. I deliver polished, production-ready work that commands attention.
